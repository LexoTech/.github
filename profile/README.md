# Lexo Technologies

**Trustworthy AI for equity research and trading.**

Lexo is an investment research workbench. It reads the filings, builds the
model and drafts the memo, and it keeps every step open to checking.

### What makes it different

- **Sourced, line by line.** Every figure and every claim links back to the page
  of the filing it came from.
- **Human judgment, not just automation.** AI does the reading and the
  arithmetic; a named analyst reviews the points that decide the call and signs
  off before anything carries the seal.
- **One run, everything you need.** The memo, the workbook model, the deck, the
  investment thesis and an audio briefing, all from the same research.
